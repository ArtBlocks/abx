# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and focus disagreement on the
work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and protect the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

Report conduct concerns privately to an Art Blocks maintainer through an established trusted channel.
Do not use public issues to disclose sensitive conduct reports.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
