Use these files when you refer to ABX or credit the infrastructure behind a project. SVG is the
preferred format for websites and interfaces. PNG is included for tools that need a raster image.

<BrandKitAssets />

## Attribution

“Built on ABX” is the clearest credit for a project, mint page, or developer tool. Choose the light
or dark lockup that has the strongest contrast. Link it to [abx.io](https://abx.io) and serve the
downloaded file from your own site.

```html
<a href="https://abx.io" aria-label="Built on ABX">
  <img src="/assets/built-on-abx-light.svg" alt="Built on ABX" width="180" height="56" />
</a>
```

Text is fine too: `Built on ABX` or `Powered by ABX`.

## Color and type

<BrandPalette />

ABX uses [Manrope](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope) for display and body copy and
[Red Hat Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Red+Hat+Mono) for code and technical labels. You do
not need either font to use the mark or attribution lockups.

## Keep it clear

- Keep the mark's proportions and give it room.
- Use black on a light surface or white on a dark surface.
- Do not rotate, outline, stretch, recolor, or add effects to the mark.
- Keep your product's identity primary. Attribution should read as infrastructure credit.
- Do not imply that ABX or Art Blocks reviewed, certified, or endorsed your project.

Need another format? Open an issue in the [ABX repository](https://github.com/ArtBlocks/abx).
