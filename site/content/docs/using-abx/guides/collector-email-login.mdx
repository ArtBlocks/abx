---
title: "Collector email login"
description: "Let collectors sign in with an email and get a wallet, using your own Privy app."
---

The [mint page](/docs/using-abx/guides/sales#mint-page) connects a collector's browser wallet. If
your collectors may not have one, you can add email login: a collector enters an email and gets an
embedded wallet automatically.

This uses a [Privy](https://privy.io) app that you create and own. ABX doesn't create the app or
hold its credentials. Privy bills your account for monthly active users beyond its free tier.
Collector wallets belong to your app. If you delete or abandon it, collectors who haven't exported
their keys lose access.

## Set up your Privy app

1. Sign in to the [Privy dashboard](https://dashboard.privy.io) and create an app. Email login is
   on by default.
2. Add every domain your collectors will use to the app's allowed domains. An app with no allowed
   domains accepts any website. Privy rejects `*.vercel.app` and `*.netlify.app` wildcards, so use a
   custom domain or the exact preview URL.
3. Switch the app to production before launch. New apps start in development mode, which caps how
   many users can sign in.
4. Copy the app ID. It's public. Your site doesn't need the app secret, so don't share it.

Check the result from the app's public configuration:

```bash
curl -s -H "privy-app-id: <app-id>" https://api.privy.io/v1/apps/<app-id>
```

`email_auth` should be `true`, and `allowed_domains` should list each collector domain.

## Add it to your site

Ask your agent to add Privy login to your mint page with your app ID. The ABX skill wires
`@privy-io/react-auth` so that:

- collectors can sign in with an email or a browser wallet;
- an embedded wallet is created for collectors who don't have one;
- Privy's confirmation prompts stay on for every signature;
- collectors can export their wallet's private key.

Keep the confirmation prompts on. Turning them off (`showWalletUIs: false`) lets your site's code
sign without the collector seeing it.

## Funding new wallets

A new email wallet is empty. Collectors need funds for the mint price and gas:

- **Crypto transfer.** Collectors send ETH to their wallet address on the collection's network. No
  setup is needed.
- **Card or bank purchase.** Enable funding methods in your Privy dashboard, and your site can open
  Privy's purchase flow.
- **Gas sponsorship (optional).** Turn on fee sponsorship in your Privy dashboard, add prepaid
  credits, and select the collection's network. Sponsorship pays gas, not the mint price, so it suits
  free mints. Your credits pay for it.

## Before launch

On the testnet and your production domain, sign in with a new email, fund the wallet, mint, and
confirm Privy's prompt appears before signing. Open the export flow once.
