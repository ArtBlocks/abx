**ABX: the X is for anything.**

Generative art got here first. It won't be the last thing. An ABX project is a programmable object that
lives onchain. It can host a website, run a game, keep time, lock up money, remember who showed up, and
fire off transactions of its own.

Every ABX project is a standard ERC-721 or ERC-1155 contract, so wallets, marketplaces, and indexers
already know what to do with it. What it holds, what it does, and who gets to change it is up to you.

## Objects that act

Most NFTs just sit there. An ABX object can do things. Attach a hook to a code project, and every mint,
transfer, burn, or setting change can set off onchain actions in the same transaction:

- Launch a token through a protocol's factory.
- Swap into or out of a pool.
- Mint a piece from another collection.
- Pay a treasury, stake, vote, or settle a position.
- Say no, and block the action, when the rules aren't met.

If a side effect fails, the whole action rolls back. Nothing half-happens. A hook is a contract you
write, so whatever a contract can do onchain, a hook can do.
[Custom mechanics](/docs/using-abx/guides/custom-mechanics) shows how.

## Live on ABX

Here's some of what people have shipped so far. Plenty of it is generative art. Plenty of it isn't.

**Money that moves**

- [Position Cards](https://arbiscan.io/address/0x6dd8e2d3ea605d6cc61f84896f967de476294b2e) (Arbitrum):
  a card with its own wallet and its own Aave loan. The art shakes harder as the loan nears
  liquidation. Survive a close call and the card keeps a crack for good.
- [HODL](https://hodl.ryley.studio) (Ethereum): lock ETH or an NFT until a date you pick. The
  certificate can't move until then, and nobody holds your funds, the creator included.
- [AB5D](https://ab5d.xyz/spot/) (Ethereum): an art exchange desk. Givers traded an AB[500] work for a
  Debit and a Spot, or left the Debit for the next giver and held a Perp that settled in turn.

**Games and live things**

- [Marfa Run Cards](https://marfa-run.vercel.app) (Base): an NES-style runner. Every run you mint is a
  card holding the course and every jump. The top score holds a soulbound crown that moves to whoever
  beats it.
- [Mio](https://basescan.org/address/0x5ccb2df3acafc9a8f59290540298de8facde2500) (Base): creatures that
  grow with every click.
- [ABXdoku](https://github.com/ArtBlocks/ABXdoku) (Base): a new Sudoku every day, with no server.
- [Marfa Light](https://etherscan.io/address/0x4504570d25b710942bee78b7ff5720950c1fc02d) (Ethereum):
  every token is a working clock for Marfa, Texas, with the real sun, moon, and stars.
- [Bird Sightings](https://arbiscan.io/address/0x73903c55c814e33762acf53dac72f75a82c83724) (Arbitrum):
  birds photographed live and identified by an agent, each logged onchain with its species code.

**Sites, radio, and social**

- [Lantern Radio](https://8453-ab025e1f9f469ae55e5f193ecdddaa1e0f4e2ff6-0.abxfs.app/) (Base, ABX FS): a
  live station with no server and no audio files. Everyone hears the same music at the same moment,
  with a chime at the top of the hour. Collectors mint tracks, poems, and cover art.
- [ABX Explorer](https://8453-6cb8206e55cf52e069fd3ed3d75b5d391934693c-1.abxfs.app/) (Base, ABX FS): an
  independent explorer for every ABX collection, itself stored onchain.
- [CoinSpace](https://coinspace.social) (Base): a retro social network. Each profile is a page with its
  own wallet and equippable widgets, and chat rooms open only to holders of a given token.
- [cc0py site](https://etherscan.io/address/0x36a34158ad7ead3b8502585136e7add92e4fb8d3) (Ethereum):
  cc0py's site for minting, browsing, and trading, published as an ABX project.

**People, places, and promises**

- [Sprite Party](https://www.spriteparty.com/) (Base): an event guestbook where every guest shows up as
  a pixel sprite.
- [GenArt Foundation donor tiles](https://www.genartfoundation.org/donors) (Base): a generative tile for
  every $100 given. Together they make the donor mural.
- [hollerith](https://thebenmeadows.com/marfa) (Ethereum): one IBM 029 punch card for every person
  TheBenMeadows met in Marfa, Texas.
- [Motivate Vows](https://basescan.org/address/0x53113a64144b5e4910b3fe9a0bb5b1f9d45001d9) (Base): a vow,
  drawn onchain. The circle closes if you kept it.
- [Desert Syntax](https://basescan.org/address/0xc4b6048ee69d445dcb63795b89121b40f085738a) (Base): a
  performance score written by a community, one page per word.
- [Grid No. 1](https://smint.xyz) (Ethereum): Vermeer's *Girl with a Pearl Earring* cut into 400
  squares. Picks stay sealed until the board closes, and each square's edition size is how many
  people picked it.

**Art by agents**

- Self-portraits minted by AI agents, including
  [Claude](https://basescan.org/address/0xf98a9e4ba5dbc5723fd21bb00f93524932b89276) and
  [Morgan](https://basescan.org/address/0xf8344172f735a5cd58c8a564880d801f13ed1114), "an agent who runs

**And yes, art**

- [Under the Influence](https://www.gifs.shop) (Ethereum), [Marfa Project](https://marfa-project.grimble.xyz/1984/)
  (Ethereum), [VOIDKA](https://voidka.art) (Base), [Polyphony](https://burntsoup.art) (Base),

## Prompts to steal

Paste one into your agent and change it until it's yours:

- "Help me put my band's website onchain and sell the album as an edition."
- "Help me make a clock for my hometown that shows the real sky."
- "Help me make a guestbook for my event where every guest gets a token."
- "Help me make a vault that locks ETH until a date, with a certificate that counts down."
- "Help me make a membership pass whose level only goes up and that can't be sold."
- "Help me make a ticket that swaps part of its mint price into our community token."
- "Help me make a pass that launches a token for its holder when they flip a switch."
- "Help me make a daily puzzle that changes at midnight UTC without a server."

Tell the agent what the thing is for, who uses it, what changes over time, and who gets to change it.
Looks come later. With the [ABX skill](/docs/using-abx/agent-and-skill), the agent explores ideas with
you and waits for you to pick one before it builds anything.

## Before you build

- These are patterns, not buttons. Rules and side effects live in a contract you write and test on a
  fork or testnet.
- Hooks attach to code projects created with `abx deploy-code`. An image series can't add them later.
- A hook spends its own funds and approvals. It never gets the holder's wallet.
- A token can stand for rights or money, but real-world rights, custody, and enforcement need their own
  legal design.
- Putting code onchain doesn't put the services it calls onchain. Plan for each one.
